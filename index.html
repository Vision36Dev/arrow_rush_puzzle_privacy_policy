Arrow Rush: Puzzle Game — Privacy Policy

Applies to the Android app com.vision36.arrow.puzzle

Effective 19 September 2026 · Last updated 19 September 2026

The short version

  • There is no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your puzzle progress is saved on your own device, not on our servers.
  • The game shows ads and reports crashes, so Google receives your device's advertising ID, basic device details and anonymous usage events.
  • You can turn off personalised ads, or delete your advertising ID entirely, from your Android settings at any time.

The rest of this page is the detailed version. It is written to match our Data safety declaration on Google Play.

1Who we are

Arrow Rush: Puzzle Game is developed and published by VISION36 ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the information described here. You can reach us at contact.feedback.store@gmail.com, or through our other games at vision36.in.

This policy covers the Arrow Rush: Puzzle Game Android app distributed through Google Play. It does not cover any other VISION36 app, and each of our other apps links its own copy of this policy from its own store listing.

2What we collect

Information you give us

None. The game has no account, no login and no contact form. If you choose to email us about a bug, we will have your email address and whatever you write — nothing more.

Information stored on your device only

Your level progress, stars, hint count and settings are written to your phone's local storage. We cannot read them, and they are removed when you uninstall the game. If you have Android Backup switched on, Google may include this file in your personal device backup under Google's own privacy policy.

Information collected automatically

Our advertising and diagnostics providers collect the following while you play:

We do not collect your precise GPS location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, files, calendar, SMS or call logs. We do not ask for those permissions.

3Services we use

These are third-party services built into the app. Each one collects data under its own privacy policy, and we cannot control what they do with it beyond the settings they give us.

  • Google AdMob — serves the ads that keep the game free. AdMob and privacy · How Google uses data from apps that use its services
  • Firebase Analytics — tells us which levels people get stuck on, so we can rebalance them. Firebase privacy and security
  • Firebase Crashlytics — sends us a report when the game crashes so we can fix it. Reports are tied to an installation, not to you.
  • Google Play services — required by Android to distribute and update the app. Google Privacy Policy

Data from AdMob is shared with Google for advertising purposes. Data from Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics is processed by Google on our behalf, for our own use.

4Why we use it

  • To show ads. Ad revenue is the only way the game pays for itself.
  • To fix crashes. A crash report tells us which line of code broke and on which device.
  • To improve levels. If ninety per cent of players quit on level 48, that level is badly tuned and we rebuild it.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, including invalid ad traffic.

We do not sell your data. We do not build advertising profiles ourselves, and we do not combine any of this with information from outside the app.

5Legal basis (UK and EU players)

  • Consent — for personalised advertising and for analytics, where required. We ask through a Google-certified consent form the first time you open the game, and you can change your answer later in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Legitimate interests — for crash reporting, security and fraud prevention, and for showing non-personalised ads where you decline consent.

6Your choices

  • Turn off personalised ads. Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID. You will still see ads, but they will be generic.
  • Change your consent answer. Open the game, go to Settings → Privacy options.
  • Opt out across Google. Google Ad Settings.
  • Remove everything. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data. To ask us to delete diagnostic data associated with your installation, email contact.feedback.store@gmail.com.

7Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to processing, or to withdraw consent. UK and EU residents hold these rights under the GDPR; California residents hold comparable rights under the CCPA, including the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them.

Email contact.feedback.store@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. Because the app has no accounts, we may need the advertising ID or app instance ID from your device to find any records at all — often there are none we can link to you. EU and UK residents may also complain to their national data protection authority.

8Children

Arrow Rush: Puzzle Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Where our ad partner detects or is told that a user is a child, ads are served in a non-personalised, child-directed mode. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, email us and we will delete it.

9How long we keep it

  • Analytics events: retained by Firebase for up to 14 months, then deleted.
  • Crash reports: retained for up to 90 days.
  • Advertising data: governed by Google's own retention schedule.
  • Local game progress: kept on your device until you uninstall or clear app data.
  • Support emails: kept for up to 24 months, then deleted.

10Security

All data leaving the app travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, but since we hold no accounts, passwords or payment details, there is very little about you for anyone to steal.

11International transfers

Google processes this data on servers in the United States and other countries. Where data leaves the UK or EEA, Google relies on Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

12Changes to this policy

If we add a feature that changes what we collect, we will update this page and move the "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be announced in the app's release notes on Google Play. Continuing to play after a change means you accept the updated policy.
